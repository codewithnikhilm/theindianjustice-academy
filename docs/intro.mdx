# Welcome to The Indian Justice Academy

This platform is built to eradicate the problem of **lack of resources in law academics**. Students and advocates from all over India should spend more time on research and studying law rather than wasting it on collecting resources.

The site contains **refined resources** for your understanding — clear notes, previous-year questions and concept breakdowns — rather than plain bare Acts left for you to decode on your own.

> Learn Law. Think Justice.
