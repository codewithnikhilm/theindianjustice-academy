# Meaning of Land

## Definition

Land includes the surface of the earth together with things attached to the earth and benefits arising out of land.

## Key Essentials

- Surface of the earth
- Trees
- Buildings
- Benefits arising out of land

## Illustration

If A sells agricultural land with a well and mango trees, these generally pass with the land.

## Important Sections

- Karnataka Land Revenue Act
- Transfer of Property Act, 1882
