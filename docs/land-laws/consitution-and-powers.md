# Chapter II: Constitution and Powers of Revenue Officers



---
## The Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964 (Sections 3 to 23)

## Part I: Constitution and Hierarchy of Revenue Officers

The structure of Revenue Officers and their chain of command begins with the State Government as the ultimate authority.

### 1. Administrative Divisions

The State Government holds the authority to determine and alter the boundaries of administrative divisions through official notifications:

* **Hierarchy:**
* **State** --> **Regions** --> **Districts** --> **Taluks** --> **Circles** --> **Villages**


* **Alteration Powers:** The State Government may alter limits, amalgamate, or constitute new villages, districts, taluks, or circles.
* **Public Notice:** When making alterations, the Government must generally publish a notice inviting public objections, unless it considers such publication unnecessary.

---

### 2. Key Revenue Officers & Subordination

| Officer | Appointment & Subordination |
| --- | --- |
| **State Government** | Chief controlling authority in all matters connected with land and land revenue administration under the Act. |
| **Regional Commissioner (RC)** | Appointed for each Region. Subject to the control of the State Government, the RC is the Chief Revenue Officer in the region and exercises superintendence and control over all subordinate officers within the region. |
| **Deputy Commissioner (DC)** | Appointed for each district. Subordinate to the Regional Commissioner. |
| **Special Deputy Commissioner (SDC)** | Appointed for a district for a specified purpose and period. Subordinate to the Regional Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner, except in matters specified by the State Government. |
| **Assistant Commissioner (AC)** | Appointed by the State Government for each district. Subordinate to the Regional Commissioner or Deputy Commissioner. An AC may be placed in charge of a Revenue Sub-Division (one or more taluks). |
| **Tahsildar** | Appointed for each taluk; serves as the Chief Officer entrusted with land revenue administration of the Taluk. Subordinate to the Assistant Commissioner (or DC if no AC is in charge). |
| **Special Tahsildar** | Appointed for any Taluk for a specified purpose and period. Subordinate to the Assistant Commissioner (or DC if no AC) and the Tahsildar, except in matters specified by the State Government. |
| **Revenue Inspector** | Appointed by the Deputy Commissioner for each circle, subject to general orders of the Regional Commissioner or State Government. |
| **Village Accountant** | Appointed by the Deputy Commissioner for a village or group of villages, subject to general orders of the State Government and Regional Commissioner. |

---

### 3. Survey Officers

The State Government appoints Survey Officers for the purposes of survey, assessments, settlements of land revenue, and boundary disputes.

#### Designated Ranks:

* Director of Survey Settlement and Land Records
* Joint Director of Land Records / Joint Director for Settlement
* Deputy Director of Land Records / Deputy Director for Settlement
* Assistant Director for Settlement / Assistant Director of Land Records
* Settlement Officers and Assistant Settlement Officers (or other designations as seen fit)

> **Note on Licensed Surveyors:** The State Government may also appoint Licensed Surveyors (possessing prescribed qualifications and experience) to prepare sketches for partitions and alienations, subject to prior State Government approval.

---

### 4. Combination of Offices & Temporary Vacancies

* **Dual Appointments:** The State Government may lawfully appoint the same person to any two or more offices provided under Chapter II, or confer upon an officer of one denomination all or any powers/duties of another officer.
* **Temporary Succession:** The Act defines temporary succession during a vacancy or disability as follows:
* **Regional Commissioner** $\rightarrow$ Deputy Commissioner stationed at headquarters.
* **Deputy Commissioner** $\rightarrow$ Special Deputy Commissioner, or the senior-most Assistant Commissioner.
* **Assistant Commissioner** $\rightarrow$ Tahsildar at headquarters.
* **Tahsildar** $\rightarrow$ Special Tahsildar or the senior-most ministerial officer.

## Part II: Powers and Duties of Revenue Officers

### 1. Functions as Revenue Courts & Inquiry Powers

* **Revenue Court Status:** A Revenue Officer not below the rank of Tahsildar is deemed a Revenue Court when exercising power under the Act to inquire into or decide questions between the State Government and any person, or between parties to any proceedings.
* **Inherent Powers:** Revenue Courts possess inherent powers to make necessary orders for the ends of justice or to prevent abuse of process.
* **Location of Proceedings:** Inquiries or hearings must generally be held within the local limits of the officer's jurisdiction (unless written reasons state otherwise or headquarters lie outside those limits).
* **Power to Take Evidence:** Revenue Officers not lower in rank than a Tahsildar or an Assistant Director of Land Records can take evidence on oath, summon parties or witnesses, and compel document production.
* **Attendance & Compulsion:** Summoned persons must attend in person or via an authorized agent (unless exempted under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908). Non-compliance may result in:
* Issuance of a bailable arrest warrant
* Order of security for appearance
* Fine not exceeding **₹20**


* **Formal vs. Summary Inquiries:**
* **Formal Inquiry:** Evidence is recorded in full with a written decision stating complete grounds.
* **Summary Inquiry:** Summary of evidence and minutes of proceedings are recorded.
* *Both inquiry types are deemed judicial proceedings under the Indian Penal Code.*


* **Power of Entry:** Officers (or authorized subordinates/workmen) may enter any land/premises for official purposes (measurement, boundary fixing, soil classification, assessment).
> **Notice Requirement:** A minimum of **7 days' prior notice** must be given before entering any dwelling house or attached courtyard/garden without the occupier's consent.



---

### 2. Case Transfer and Administration

* **Transfer of Cases:**
* **Tribunal:** May transfer cases between Regional Commissioners.
* **Regional Commissioner:** May transfer cases between competent Revenue Officers within the same region or district.
* **Deputy Commissioner:** May transfer cases between subordinate officers or withdraw cases to their own file.


* **Delegation by Tahsildars:** Tahsildars/Special Tahsildars may depute subordinates to perform ministerial duties, subject to revision and confirmation.
* **Village Accountant Duties:** Must maintain prescribed registers, accounts, and records, and prepare all village-related documents required by superior officers or the Government.
* **Official Seals:** Specified Revenue Officers must use an official seal of prescribed size and description.

---

### 3. Recovery of Public Money and Property

* **Demand Authority:** The DC, Joint Director of Land Records, or Joint Director for Settlement may demand public money, papers, or government property detained by any current or former Revenue Officer.
* **Enforcement & Civil Jail:** If non-compliant, the DC or Joint Director may apprehend and confine the individual in civil jail for up to **90 days**.
* **Recovery Mechanism:** Public moneys due may be recovered using the procedures applicable to recovering land revenue arrears.
* **Search Warrants:** The DC may issue search warrants to recover public papers or property, exercising powers equivalent to a Magistrate under the Code of Criminal Procedure.

---

### 4. Eviction and Land Administration Powers

* **Eviction Procedure:** Involves serving a notice to vacate, removing refusers using subordinate officers, and—if resistance occurs without just cause—holding a summary inquiry and using reasonable force to secure compliance.
* **Summary Eviction (Tahsildar):** The Tahsildar may summarily evict any unauthorized occupant of non-transferable land governed by conditional tenure (under Sections 91, 98, or 99).
* **Forfeiture (Tahsildar):** The Tahsildar may forfeit an occupancy or alienated holding to the State Government for non-payment of land revenue.
> **Restriction:** Forfeiture is **prohibited** if arrears do not exceed **₹10,000**.


* **Recovery of Value:** The Tahsildar determines the value of any natural product unauthorizedly removed from government property.
* **Disposal of Land:** Subject to prescribed rules, State Government property/land may be disposed of by:
* State Government
* Regional Commissioner
* Deputy Commissioner
* Assistant Commissioner (in charge of Taluk/Taluks)
* Tahsildar
